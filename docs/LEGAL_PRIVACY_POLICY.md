# Privacy Policy — Exploro

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 11, 2026  
**Application:** Exploro (Web, iOS, Android)

Exploro is committed to protecting your privacy and upholding absolute transparency in how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy details our data practices across our mobile and web applications, strictly adhering to our core principle: **Zero Commercial Sale of Personal Data** and **Strict Ethical Evidence Collection**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information (Optional)
Exploro allows full exploratory browsing without an account (*Guest Mode*). If you choose to create an account, we collect:
- Email address (for authentication and password recovery).
- Display name (visible when submitting community feedback).
- Hashed password credentials (secured via Argon2id / SHA-256 cryptographic salts; plain-text passwords are never stored or transmitted).

### 1.2 User Preferences & Itineraries
- Travel preferences (budget tier, travel styles, companion types, favorite categories).
- Custom trips, itineraries, bookmarks, and collections saved to your profile.

### 1.3 Voluntary Geolocation Data
- **High-Precision GPS:** Used **only with your explicit permission** when:
  - Finding nearby places in real-time.
  - Voluntarily verifying physical presence at a location (*Optional Visit Verification*).
- **No Background Tracking:** Exploro **never** tracks your location in the background or when the app is closed.

### 1.4 Community Reviews & Content Reports
- Structured responses regarding taste, service, value, duration, and recommendations.
- Textual comments and voluntary tips. Under Exploro’s immutable governance policy, user opinions cannot be altered by administrative staff.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively to:
1. Provide personalized discovery and itinerary generation tailored to your explicit preferences.
2. Calculate walking and driving route summaries locally and via open-source routing (OSRM).
3. Detect fraudulent, automated, or manipulated reviews.
4. Maintain system security, auditability, and service availability.

---

## 3. Strict Zero-Sale and Zero Third-Party Advertising Policy

- **We DO NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.**
- **We DO NOT use commercial ad networks or third-party behavioral tracking SDKs** (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or tracking pixels).
- **Map & Geocoding Privacy:** Map vector tiles and geocoding queries are powered by open-source infrastructure (MapLibre GL and OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap). Your search queries and locations are not tied to commercial advertising profiles.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Security

- **Encryption in Transit & at Rest:** All communications use TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords and session tokens use industry-standard hashing and rotating JWTs.
- **Data Sovereignty:** You have the absolute right to export or delete your account and associated data at any time via your account settings.

---

## 5. Compliance with US and International Privacy Standards

- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA):** California residents have the right to request deletion, access, and know what personal information is collected. As noted, Exploro does not sell personal information.
- **Children's Privacy (COPPA):** Exploro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
