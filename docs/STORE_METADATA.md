**Target Markets:** United States (Initial Wave: NYC, SFO, CHI; Secondary Wave: MIA, LAX, ATX, SEA)  
**Supported Platforms:** Apple App Store (iOS/iPadOS) & Google Play Store (Android Phones & Tablets)

  Discover genuine food, culture, and places backed by evidence. No fake ratings.

### 1.2 Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

```text
Tired of sponsored tourist traps, inflated review scores, and fabricated travel guides?

Meet Exploro: the evidence-based discovery platform designed for curious travelers who value genuine experiences.

WHY EXPLORO IS DIFFERENT

1. EVIDENCE-BASED, NEVER INVENTED
Every recommendation in Exploro is backed by verified evidence from real local sources, traveler journals, and genuine community feedback. If a schedule or price tier is unconfirmed, we label it clearly. No guesswork. No hallucinated places.

2. TAILORED FOR EXPLORATION
Choose "I'm Traveling" mode and instantly discover authentic food spots, cultural gems, scenic viewpoints, and local activities tailored to your budget, companion type, and travel style.

3. SMART ITINERARY PLANNER
Plan single or multi-day journeys effortlessly. Reorganize activities with realistic walking and transit times, explore alternative spots in 3 smart tiers (Very Similar, Related Experiences, or High-Reward Adjustments), and customize schedules without stress.

4. REAL COMMUNITY FEEDBACK
Read structured feedback from verified visits. Exploro strictly preserves traveler opinions: no business or administrator can alter reviews.

5. FAST, BEAUTIFUL & PRIVACY-FIRST
Powered by open-source vector maps, ultra-fast local spatial queries, and zero commercial ad-network tracking. Your location is used only when you choose to find nearby spots or verify a visit.

KEY FEATURES:
• 6 Core Categories: Food & Dining, Tourism & Landmarks, Entertainment, Activities, Events, and Shopping.
• 7 Specialized Tourism subcategories: Scenic Viewpoints, Monuments, Nature & Parks, Museums, Cultural Hubs, Towns & Districts, and Emblematic Landmarks.
• Interactive Map with OpenFreeMap & MapLibre GL.
• "Surprise Me" smart discovery for spontaneous outings.
• Save favorites and custom collections.
• Offline caching: browse saved trips even with limited connectivity.

Explore genuinely with Exploro.
```

## 3. United States City Launch Strategy

Exploro launches strategically with a city-by-city rollout plan focused on high-density cultural and culinary destinations:

### Phase A: Core Metropolitan Launch (Initial Wave)
1. **New York City, NY (`new-york`):**
   - High density of culinary and cultural diversity.
   - Comprehensive seed places across Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Queens.
2. **San Francisco, CA (`san-francisco`):**
   - Iconic landmarks, nature, and scenic viewpoints (Twin Peaks, Golden Gate, Mission).
3. **Chicago, IL (`chicago`):**
   - Architecture, lakefront parks, museums, and neighborhood dining.

### Phase B: Regional Expansion Wave
4. **Miami, FL (`miami`):** Coastal culture, art deco, Latin cuisine.
5. **Los Angeles, CA (`los-angeles`):** Distinct neighborhoods, arts, and culinary hubs.
6. **Austin, TX (`austin`):** Live music, BBQ, outdoor recreation.
7. **Seattle, WA (`seattle`):** Pacific Northwest nature, coffee culture, waterfront markets.
